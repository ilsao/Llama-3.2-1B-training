# Understanding Warmup-Stable-Decay Learning Rates: A River Valley Loss Landscape Perspective

Kaiyue Wen, Zhiyuan Li, Jason Wang, David Hall, Percy Liang, Tengyu Ma. arXiv:2410.05192v3,2024.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05192v3

The paper describes branching from a constant-LR main trajectory and lowering LR to reduce oscillation near a valley floor. This motivates sharing a stable checkpoint for matched-horizon cooldown timing comparisons. Its WSD-S proposal resumes from decayed checkpoints, motivating our final reheating comparison without implying that the exact5/6–1/6 recipe or inherited exponent0.32 is optimal. Our existing hold/zero shape is retained as a controlled variant; evaluate completed endpoints and separate stable-phase training loss from validation quality.
